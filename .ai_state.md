  + `visual/paradigm_nominal{.json,_data.js}` + two reports +
  [`tests/test_nominal_dashboard.js`](https://github.com/gasyoun/VisualDCS/blob/main/tests/test_nominal_dashboard.js).
  What future-you must know before touching this:
  1. **The complement of a `feat_case IN (…) AND feat_number IN (…)` filter must be spelled out
     NULL-safely.** Writing it as `NOT (… AND …)` silently loses every case-untagged row: SQL's
     three-valued logic makes `NULL IN (…)` → `NULL`, `NULL AND TRUE` → `NULL`, `NOT NULL` →
     `NULL`, so 8,542 tokens matched neither the grid query nor its supposed complement and
     disappeared from both denominators. The generator now asserts
     grid + `Cpd` + unplaceable == the whole NOUN/ADJ universe (2,996,410) and exits non-zero
     otherwise — that assertion is what caught it. Any new per-slice query here needs the same
     closure check.
  2. **Declension class is NOT a DCS tag** and must never be presented as one. The class axis is
     an orthographic heuristic over the lemma citation form, reusing SanskritGrammar Sangram
     G2's tag list (H1048) so the two assets stay comparable — do not invent a second taxonomy.
     The only local extension is `-ant` (279 lemma_ids pulled out of G2's `other_consonant`),
     and every re-bucketing is counted in `reports/nominal_g2_reconciliation.md`.
  3. **`feat_case='Cpd'` is not a ninth case** — 724,676 tokens (~32% of the nominal mass) that
     have no case at all. They stay out of the 24-cell grid and are reported separately; never
     quietly fold them into a "case distribution".
  4. **A gender column is not a gender paradigm.** The axis is the *token's* tagged gender, so
     feminine tokens of `-a`-cited adjectives (`paramayā`) sit in the `-a` class's Fem column
     inflecting as ā-/ī-stems. Reading that column as "the a-stem feminine paradigm" is wrong.
  5. **Reconciliation is exact and worth re-running as a regression:** 57,144 lemma_ids agree
     with G2 on both token and attested-cell counts, 0 disagreements.
  6. **Two roadmap.md numbers do not reproduce on DCS-2026:** Nom.Sg is 33.7% of cased tokens
     (roadmap says 34.6%, from the 2021 `cs.csv`), and "Dual < 1% everywhere" holds only per
     cell (max 0.91%) — pooled, the dual is 2.07%. The roadmap banner now records this.

  What future-you must know before touching this:
  1. **The two corpus files differ from MW by TWO orthographic conventions that must be folded or the
     join is fake:** DCS writes anusvāra as `ṁ` (U+1E41, dot-above); MW writes `ṃ` (U+1E43, dot-below).
     MW keys also carry `@`/`-` markup and a leading avagraha `'` (= elided initial *a*: `'bja`=abja).
     Naïve string-join matches only 29 %; after folding, 33 %. `okey()` in the build script folds these.
  2. **Do NOT fold vowel-length / gender / junction sandhi** (`sena`/`senā`, `vatī`/`vat`, `cchada`/`chada`):
     those are morphological citation-form differences (DCS lemmatises to stem), diagnosed as
     `form_variant`, not absence. 1,289 such — never assert them corpus-absent.
  3. **cmps.csv (399,096 forms) ⊇ names.csv (168,481 with DCS freq), verified.** cmps = form universe,
     names = frequency annotation by surface. corpus_tokens sums only the freq-annotated subset.
  4. **Finding:** MW and the corpus are productive over the *same* finals but with near-disjoint
     first-member sets (median Jaccard 0, 56 % zero-overlap). The 10,387 "dictionary-only" finals are
     86 % MW-hapax — corpus-unattested ≠ ghost (SanskritLexicography FINDINGS §86). Corpus side has its
     own junk head (enclitic `ca` = 11,057 forms / 2 tokens; pronoun stems; bare roots; the `-tva`/`-tā`
     taddhita suffixes MW excluded) — filtered by tokens + explicit stoplist in Direction B.
  5. **Temporal (early/late) cut deliberately NOT done:** the per-period columns in `names.csv` have no
     documented column→period mapping (Kompozity README), so it would be guesswork.
