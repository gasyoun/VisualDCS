## Verdict — a bounded re-split, with the bounds stated

The merged `Perfect/Aorist` bucket is gone, and most of the split is read off the corpus rather than guessed. But the split is **partial**, and both classes are bounds:

- **Aorist = 12,054 is a LOWER bound.** It counts only formation-tagged aorists; at least 869 more sit untagged inside the Perfect class.
- **Perfect = 81,275 is an UPPER bound.** It carries ≥869 misfiled aorists and ≥2,733 misfiled imperfects — ≥4.66% known contamination of the defaulted class.
- **Neither number is exact, and neither should be quoted as exact.** Quote them as bounds, or quote the tagged-only figures with their coverage.

What was *superseded*: the claim that `feat_formation` is "present on <2% of verbs, too sparse to re-split them". The arithmetic was right and the denominator was wrong — 16,100 tags against ~1.01M verbs of every kind is 1.60%, but the tag only ever applies to the finite past indicative, where it covers **17.25%** and resolves 12,054 aorists plus 4,046 periphrastic perfects. Sparse against the wrong denominator is not sparse.
